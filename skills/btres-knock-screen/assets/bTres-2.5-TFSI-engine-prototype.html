2.5 TFSI knock screen prototype

A 1920×480 data screen for S-series bTres displays on the 2.5 TFSI five-cylinder. Values are simulated through repeated pulls. When a cylinder pulls more timing than the alert threshold, the engine turns toward that side, the cylinder glows and its callout lights up.

Cylinders

Orange glow means a cylinder is pulling timing. Red glow and a callout mean it passed the alert threshold. K is the knock count for the current pull, IGN the current timing correction, LAST the previous pull.
